🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard
1. Project Title / Headline
🍕 Pizza Analytics: Interactive Sales Performance Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze pizza sales data, uncover revenue trends, evaluate product performance, and generate actionable business insights through dynamic visualizations.
2. Short Description / Purpose
The Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard is a two-page interactive Power BI report developed to monitor key business performance indicators and understand customer ordering patterns. It focuses on analyzing total revenue, order volume, pizza category and size preferences, daily and monthly sales trends, and best- and worst-selling products.
The dashboard is intended for business owners, sales analysts, and decision-makers seeking to understand sales performance, identify product trends, and support data-driven business decisions.
3. Tech Stack
The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:
- 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main business intelligence and data visualization platform used to create the interactive dashboard.
- 📁 Power Query – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation before analysis.
- 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create calculated measures and KPIs, including total revenue, average order value, total orders, and average pizzas per order.
- 📈 Data Visualization – Used to represent sales trends, category contributions, product rankings, and performance metrics.
- 🎨 Dashboard Design – Used to organize KPIs, charts, slicers, and business insights into an interactive two-page report.
4. Data Source
The project uses a Pizza Sales Dataset containing transactional information about pizza orders, products, pricing, and quantities.
The dataset includes the following fields:
- Order ID
- Order Date and Time
- Pizza Name
- Pizza Category
- Pizza Size
- Quantity Sold
- Unit Price
- Total Sales / Revenue
The dataset serves as the foundation for analyzing sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product-level trends.
5. Features & Highlights
A. Business Problem
Pizza businesses generate large volumes of transactional data, making it difficult to monitor overall performance, identify popular products, understand customer preferences, and recognize sales patterns.
This dashboard addresses these challenges by presenting key sales metrics and product performance insights in a centralized, interactive reporting environment.
B. Dashboard Pages
Page 1: Sales Overview
- High-level business performance monitoring.
- Daily order trends across the week.
- Monthly order trends throughout the year.
- Revenue contribution by pizza category.
- Sales distribution by pizza size.
- Total pizzas sold by category.
- Interactive pizza category and date filters.
- Business insights highlighting peak sales days and months.
Page 2: Best & Worst Sellers
- Top 5 pizzas by revenue.
- Top 5 pizzas by quantity sold.
- Top 5 pizzas by total orders.
- Bottom 5 pizzas by revenue.
- Bottom 5 pizzas by quantity sold.
- Bottom 5 pizzas by total orders.

D. Business Insights
- Identified Friday and Saturday as peak ordering days.
- Observed higher monthly order activity during January and July.
- Analyzed Classic pizza category contribution to overall sales.
- Evaluated pizza size preferences, with Large pizzas contributing the highest sales share.
- Compared top-performing and low-performing pizzas across revenue, quantity, and order volume.
E. Interactive Features
- Date range slicer for analyzing selected periods.
- Pizza category filter for focused analysis.
- Interactive charts and KPI cards.
- Cross-filtering between report visualizations.
- Separate report pages for sales overview and product performance.
F. Business Impact
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance, helping users identify revenue patterns, evaluate product demand, monitor KPIs, and explore opportunities for inventory planning and sales optimization.
